## Interpretation

The template-generated cases check policy consistency. The challenge cases test
negation, conflicting evidence, and missing evidence; they are still synthetic.
Neither set establishes learning gains, retention, conversion, or real student
behavior. See `docs/evaluation-methodology.md` and `docs/limitations.md`.
