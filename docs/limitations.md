# Limitations and Evidence Boundaries

- The 200 cases are synthetic and generated from documented templates.
- The current diagnosis module is rule-based, not a trained or fine-tuned model.
- Metrics measure offline policy behavior, not real learning outcomes.
- No claim is made about user retention, transfer-test pass rate, NPS, latency,
  concurrency, revenue, or production adoption.
- The first-turn answer-exposure comparator is intentionally naive and exists to
  make the policy difference inspectable.
- Chinese learner language outside the provided patterns may route to
  clarification more often.
- There is no teacher console, school account system, payment flow, or RBAC.
- AI-assisted coding was used; product definition, workflow decisions,
  evaluation design, and result verification are the primary contribution.
